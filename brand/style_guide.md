# Summerfield Tea Bar - style guide for training videos and job aids

The planner, the director (review loop) and the instruction-card writer all read this file.
`update-guideline` regenerates the guideline section from a new PDF; `learn-example`
regenerates the video-template section from an approved video. Edit freely in between.

## From the 2026 brand guideline

**Feel.** Warm, elevated, inviting, visually immersive. Clean compositions with emotional,
lifestyle-driven storytelling: modern, intentional, easy to connect with. Comfort, connection
and everyday moments - never overly promotional or heavily designed.

**Visual direction.** Clean and minimal · warm and atmospheric · modern and youthful · soft and
natural · visually balanced · emotionally engaging.

**Palette.**
| Role | Name | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Light sage (backgrounds) | PANTONE 9042 C | #E3E8E3 |
| Sage green (stripes, accents) | PANTONE 2260 C | #B6CFAE |
| Soft blue | PANTONE 643 C | #BFD3D6 |
| Peach | PANTONE 9220 C | #F2DBC7 |
| Caramel (pointers, warm accents) | PANTONE 728 C | #CDA077 |
| Near-black (text) | PANTONE Black 7 C | #231F20 |
| White | - | #FFFFFF |

**Typography.**
- Caudex (primary): headlines, featured titles, key brand moments.
- Work Sans (secondary): subtitles, body copy, labels, longer informational text.
- Côte Lumière (complementary script): short headlines, featured phrases, accent typography
  only. Never for long text.
- Use the three typefaces in a clear hierarchy; avoid incorrect combinations.

**Logo.** Primary stacked logo, logomark, stampmark, logotype and horizontal lockup. Never
oversized, never low-contrast, never covering important photo elements.

**Layout rules.**
- Avoid excessive typography, graphics or unnecessary elements - the imagery is the focus.
- Text short and intentional; extra detail belongs in captions/notes, not on screen.
- Never put text or shadows over busy photography; use a clean area or a label.
- No gradients behind information.
- Don't cover important parts of the photo with labels.
- Keep air and negative space; compositions never crowded.

**Photography.** Clean, elevated, natural light, subtle depth, realistic textures, not
over-staged or commercial. Lifestyle shots: authentic, candid, warm. Always real photos -
no illustrations standing in for the product or the process.

## Video template (from the approved back-of-house training sample)

**Overall.** 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Calm narrated voiceover over every scene (one voice,
no music), short silences between lines. Soft cross-dissolves between scenes. Elements
appear one after another with gentle eased motion, never all at once and never hard pops.

**Persistent chrome.**
- "CONFIDENTIAL MATERIAL" badge, top-right, muted mauve pill, white letter-spaced caps.
- Key-point caption, bottom centre, mauve-grey pill (#93817E), white Work Sans, one short line.
  On cards it sits on a full-width striped sage band (#B6CFAE / #DBE3D7).

**Footage scenes.**
- Top-left strip: "STEP 1A | MEASURE THE WATER" - green step code, letter-spaced caps,
  translucent sage background with a green left bar.
- Under it, on the first shot of a sub-step only: title tag with a green rounded badge
  ("1A") and the sub-step name in Caudex.
- Top-right: up to three stacked callout boxes with a white border - green for what to use /
  do, red for "NEVER", light green for times and info, tan pill for short notes
  ("SMOOTH · NO LUMPS").
- Picture-in-picture inset with a white rounded frame to zoom on small details (scale
  readout, fill line, label).
- Tan arrow + label pointing at the exact spot ("PRESS TARE", "LABEL HERE", "BOTTOM SHELF").

**Cards.** Light sage background, small eyebrow top-left, Caudex heading, letter-spaced
subheading with a green bar, content in white rounded panels with soft shadows.
- Title card: horizontal logo, large Caudex title, series label, mascot, real photo of the
  finished result on a soft green panel.
- Overview: numbered step thumbnails joined by caramel arrows.
- What You Need: equipment list + ingredients with exact amounts (both units).
- Food Safety First / storage rules: numbered tiles (kicker, rule, note) revealed one by one.
- Chapter divider: "STEP 2 OF 4", Caudex title, progress pills (current step filled green),
  photo of that step.
- Right vs Wrong: two real photos, red WRONG frame with an X, green RIGHT frame with a check,
  short label pills.
- Closing: logo, mascot, celebratory word, photo of the finished product.

**Pacing.** Each scene carries one idea, about 3-8 s. Footage clips show exactly what is
being said. Whole video under 6 minutes - shorter whenever nothing essential is lost.
